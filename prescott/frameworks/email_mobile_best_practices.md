# Email Best Practices — Mobile Promotional Emails (DTC)

## Scroll Depth

Industry best practice for DTC promotional emails on mobile is **1 to 2 scrolls maximum**.

- The average recipient decides within **3 seconds** whether to keep reading or delete
- Your headline and CTA should both be visible within the **first scroll** — meaning if someone never scrolls at all, they still see the offer and know how to act on it
- Supporting details live in the **second scroll** at most
- Anything beyond 2 scrolls on a promotional email sees significant drop-off in engagement

## The Golden Rule

**One message. One offer. One button. Everything else is noise.**

## Email Audit Checklist — Mobile Layout

Use the following when auditing or reviewing any DTC promotional email:

- [ ] Is the headline visible within the first scroll on mobile?
- [ ] Is the primary CTA button visible within the first scroll on mobile?
- [ ] Does the email communicate a single clear offer or message?
- [ ] Is there only one primary CTA button (not multiple competing buttons)?
- [ ] Is the email 2 scrolls or fewer on a standard mobile screen?
- [ ] Is the copy concise — no paragraph longer than 2-3 sentences?
- [ ] Does the subject line match the email's main offer or message?
- [ ] Is the hero image relevant and high quality?

## Email Creation Guidelines

Apply the following when building any new DTC promotional email:

- **Structure:** Hero image → Short headline (5 words or fewer) → 1 sentence body copy → CTA button → Supporting details (optional, scroll 2)
- **Copy length:** Less is always more. If you can say it in one sentence, do not use two.
- **CTA:** One primary button, high contrast, action-oriented label (BUY NOW, SHOP NOW, CLAIM OFFER)
- **Images:** Lifestyle over product shots where possible — show the experience, not just the item
- **Subject line:** 35-45 characters, benefit or curiosity driven, no clickbait
- **Preview text:** Extends the subject line, never repeats it word for word
