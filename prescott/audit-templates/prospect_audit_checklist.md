# Prospect Audit Checklist — No Platform Access

How to build a credible email/retention audit for a brand you don't have Klaviyo/ESP access to. Everything here is observable from the outside. Budget 45-60 min per brand.

Use a real inbox you'll actually monitor for 2 weeks (not a throwaway) — you need to receive the full welcome series and campaign cadence to audit it properly.

## 1. Signup capture
- Sign up for their email list from the homepage (not a landing page — you want the default experience).
- Note: does a popup fire immediately, on scroll, or on exit-intent? What's the headline offer (% off, $ off, free gift, nothing)?
- Note: is there a separate SMS opt-in? Same offer or different?

## 2. Welcome series (highest-value flow to audit — always do this one)
- Log every email that arrives over the next 10 days: send time relative to signup, subject line, offer, CTA.
- Per the Playbook, benchmark is 3-4 emails over 5-10 days, escalating from brand story → education/social proof → purchase incentive.
- Flag: single confirmation email only = no real welcome series. That's a Tier 1 finding.

## 3. Browse abandonment
- Visit 3-4 product pages while cookied/logged in (use the same email you signed up with if the site supports account creation). Leave without adding to cart.
- Wait 24-48 hours. Did you get an email referencing those specific products?
- Most mid-market brands don't have this — expect to find it missing. That's fine, it's still worth naming as an opportunity.

## 4. Cart abandonment
- Add 1-2 items to cart, get to the point where email/info is captured, then abandon.
- Log: time to first email, total number of emails, discount ladder (does the offer increase across the sequence — that's a red flag per the Playbook's promo rules), whether the specific abandoned product appears in the email.

## 5. Checkout abandonment (if distinct from cart)
- If the site has guest checkout, get further than step 3 (payment info page) before abandoning.
- Some ESPs fire a separate sequence here with stronger urgency. Note if it's distinct from the cart flow or identical (identical = missed opportunity).

## 6. Campaign cadence (needs the 2-week window)
- Count campaign emails received in 14 days, separate from flow emails.
- Note variety: does every email look like a discount blast, or is there a content/education mix?
- Any sign of segmentation (e.g., an email clearly tailored to a category you browsed vs. a generic full-list send)?

## 7. Copy & offer hygiene (apply Playbook's Promotional Hard Rules)
- Does every promo answer: what is this / why does it matter / why act now?
- Any false urgency (countdown timers that reset, "today only" that repeats)?
- Discount escalation across the cart flow — trains customers to wait, worth flagging as a finding, not just an observation.

## 8. Deliverability & personalization signals
- Landing in Promotions tab or Spam?
- First-name personalization present?
- Unsubscribe link present and working (basic list hygiene check)?

## 9. Site + social signals (context, not core findings)
- Exit-intent popup, loyalty/rewards program, subscription option — note what exists.
- Reviews platform (Trustpilot/Judge.me/Yotpo) — review count and rating gives a rough read on scale and trust signals.
- Instagram/TikTok follower count and engagement rate — rough proxy for audience size, useful for sizing the opportunity slide.

## 10. Maturity tier estimate
Use the Lifecycle Playbook's brand maturity framework to place them, based on what you've observed (review count, product catalog size, ad presence, site polish):
- **$1-3M:** thin flow coverage, single welcome email, minimal segmentation — pitch education + foundational flows.
- **$5-30M:** flows exist but gaps in behavioral segmentation, promo-heavy — pitch segmentation architecture + value-tier work.
- **$50M+:** likely already has an agency — pitch needs to be sharper and more specific to win the audit call at all.

This tier estimate drives which engagement pitch you use on the call (see `cold_outreach_email_sequence.md`, Call Script section).
