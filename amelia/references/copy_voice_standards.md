# Copy & Voice Standards

Load this file before writing any copy — headlines, body, CTAs, subject lines, preheaders.

---

## Casing — Firm Defaults, Brand Overrides Apply

- **Headlines:** Title Case by default. Some brands use ALL CAPS — read brand.md and match. Never mix cases in a single email.
- **Body copy:** Sentence case always. No exceptions.
- **CTAs:** ALL CAPS by default (`SHOP NOW`, `GET YOUR PASS`). Confirm per brand — some use Title Case. Match what's established.
- Never use Title Case in body copy. Never use ALL CAPS in body copy outside of labels and CTAs.

## Register

**Punchy is the default.** Short, declarative, often a question or claim. Sentence fragments are acceptable and often preferred — "Race day ready." reads better than "You are ready for race day." Cut every word not pulling weight.

**Second person, present tense, active voice.** Speak directly to the subscriber: "you," "your," "you're." Active verbs. "Your horse feels the difference" beats "The difference will be felt by your horse."

**Voice flexes per brand — always read brand assets before writing copy.** Match the register — formal vs casual, aspirational vs direct, warm vs confident.

## Numbers and Specifics Beat Adjectives

"4 custom labels" beats "beautiful custom labels." "200 points to start" beats "a generous loyalty bonus." "500mg of glutathione" beats "a powerful dose." When there is a number, use it. When there isn't, find the specific detail.

## CTAs — Verb-Led, 2–4 Words

Start every CTA with a verb. `SHOP EARLY ACCESS` / `JOIN THE CLUB` / `GET YOUR PASS` / `CLAIM YOUR BUNDLE` / `EXPLORE THE COLLECTION`. Two to four words. Never a passive CTA — "Learn More" says nothing about what happens next.

## Copy Length

- Intro paragraph: 2 sentences max
- Feature card body: 1–2 sentences
- If it takes more words than that, the message is not clear enough

## Placeholders

If copy is not yet written, write tight placeholder copy that reads as if it could ship. Flag every placeholder clearly: `[PLACEHOLDER: confirm offer headline here]`. Never leave a blank field.

Never invent prices, claims, ingredients, or product names. If a price is not confirmed: `[PRICE TBC]`. If a product name is not confirmed: `[PRODUCT NAME TBC]`.
