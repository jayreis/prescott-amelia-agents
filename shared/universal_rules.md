# Universal Rules — All Agents, All Clients, All Platforms

These rules apply across every agent (Amelia, Prescott), every client, every platform (Klaviyo, Omnisend, Constant Contact), and every deliverable. They are not defaults — they are non-negotiable.

---

## NX Prefix — Platform Assets

Every campaign, flow, list, segment, or template created or renamed in any client's ESP must be prefixed with `NX - `.

Examples: `NX - Welcome Series`, `NX - July 4th Sale`, `NX - High-Value Buyers`

Applies to: Klaviyo, Omnisend, Constant Contact. No exceptions by platform or client.

---

## No Emoji — Anywhere

No emoji in any deliverable:
- Subject lines
- Preheaders
- Headlines
- Body copy
- CTAs
- Footer text
- Asana task names or comments
- Brief copy direction

If a brief, client request, or prior template includes emoji — remove them before proceeding.

---

## No Lorem Ipsum — Ever

If copy has not been provided, write tight placeholder copy that reads as if it could ship — correct tone, correct length, correctly positioned.

Flag every placeholder clearly: `[PLACEHOLDER: Jay to confirm offer headline here]`

Never leave lorem ipsum. Never leave a blank copy field in any deliverable.

---

## No Em Dashes

Em dashes (`—`) are not permitted in any copy that ships to subscribers or clients. Rewrite the sentence to avoid the em dash, or use a hyphen-space combo where a pause is needed.

This applies to: subject lines, preheaders, body copy, headlines, CTAs, Asana comments, and any client-facing output.

---

## No Marketing-Speak

Banned words and phrases across all copy:
`revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `unlock`, `elevate`, `seamless`, `robust`, `leverage` (unless literal), `empower`, `synergy`, `ecosystem`, `innovative`, `cutting-edge`, `best-in-class`, `world-class`, `disruptive`, `holistic`

If a word appears on every DTC brand's website homepage, do not use it. Write with specificity instead.

---

## One Email, One Message, One CTA Destination

Every email communicates exactly one thing and drives exactly one action. If a brief has competing messages, it becomes two briefs. If a brief has two unrelated CTA destinations, it becomes two emails.

The CTA button may repeat 2–3 times down the page, but all instances point to the same destination family.

---

## Never Invent Facts

No fabricated prices, product names, benefit claims, statistics, review counts, or ingredient details — in any brief, any email, any report.

If a fact is not confirmed from the client's own materials: flag it with `[TBC]` and surface it for Jay to verify. This rule has zero tolerance — fabricated specifics can ship to thousands of subscribers or go to clients.
