## Colors
- **Accent:** #FF6900 — CTAs, badges, highlights

- **Light text:** #FFFFFF

## Typography
- Headlines: Title Case
- CTAs: ALL CAPS

## Voice
- Direct, benefit-led, no hype language
- Second person, active voice

## Don't Do
- No emoji
- No em dashes
- No unverified claims or statistics
